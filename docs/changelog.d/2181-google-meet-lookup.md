- **Google Meet lookup-link dispatch (TC-2181).** `POST /bots` derives the literal `lookup/<nickname>` native meeting key from `https://meet.google.com/lookup/<nickname>` when `native_meeting_id` is omitted or JSON null. Different nicknames stay distinct; repeat submissions share the same deduplication key. Explicit `lookup/<nickname>` IDs construct the corresponding Meet URL. Nicknames preserve case and accept only ASCII letters, digits, `_`, and `-`, with a 255-character maximum for the complete key; malformed lookup paths are refused. Dashed meeting codes retain their existing behavior. Native-key stop, transcript, and meeting routes accept these path-shaped keys through both gateway and meeting-api (clients may percent-encode the key's slash). This fixes dispatch/addressing, not Google admission policy; a live Google Meet capture has not been validated for this change.
