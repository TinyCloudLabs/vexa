- **Meeting bot: `needs_help` is accepted while joining (TC-1830).** A Google Meet bot that needs human help before admission now reports `needs_help` from the `joining` state, instead of only after it reaches `awaiting_admission`. Joining-state escalations are held until 240 seconds after join start and are dropped if the bot has already moved on. The `needs_help` event carries `escalation_reason`, one of `modal_dialog`, `consent_text`, `notes_text`, `waiting_room_indicator`, `no_positive_evidence`, or `unknown`. Poll-path reasons (`waiting_room_timeout_approaching`, `unknown_blocking_state`, `join_error_page_alive`) are unchanged. This mechanism is proven offline only; a live Google Meet validation is still open.
